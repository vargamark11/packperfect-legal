Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At PackPerfect, your privacy is our highest priority. Our application is architected from the ground up to be private, local, and offline-first.

  • No User Tracking: PackPerfect does not collect, sell, or share any personal information, behavioral data, or usage metrics.
  • 100% Local Storage: All your trips, custom packing checklists, notes, companion assignments, and preferences are stored exclusively on your device using Apple SwiftData.
  • No Third-Party Analytics or Ads: There are zero advertising networks, tracking SDKs, or third-party cookies embedded in the app.
  • In-App Purchases: All purchases (PackPerfect Pro) are processed securely and directly by Apple via StoreKit. We never see or store your payment details.
  • Data Deletion: You have complete control over your data. You can erase all saved trips and data directly inside the app at any time via Settings → Legal → Account and Data Deletion.

Support & Contact

Have questions, suggestions, or need assistance with PackPerfect? We are here to help you get the best packing experience for your travels.

  • Bug Reports & Feedback: Tell us about any issues or feature requests.
  • Restore Purchases: PackPerfect Pro is a one-time purchase. If you reinstall the app or change devices, use the "Restore Purchases" button in Settings to unlock your features instantly.
